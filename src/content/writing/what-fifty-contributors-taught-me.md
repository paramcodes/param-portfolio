title: what 50+ contributors taught me about code review

As a GSSoC '24 project admin I led 50+ contributors and merged 50+ PRs while keeping zero-downtime deploys. The technical lesson was predictable — CI discipline, small diffs. The human one wasn't: most review friction is ambiguity about what "good" means, not skill.

This post covers the review habits that made the queue move: stating the why behind every requested change, separating blocking from optional feedback, and writing the first comment before the first push. Full write-up coming December 2026.
